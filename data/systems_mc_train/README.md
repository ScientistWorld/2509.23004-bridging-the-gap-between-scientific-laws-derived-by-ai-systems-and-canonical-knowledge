# Larger train theories for the multiple-consequence experiment

Public extensions of four of the paper's benchmark theories (Kepler, escape
velocity, simple harmonic oscillator, time dilation), each with two to four more
physical laws (orbital energy and angular momentum, circular-orbit speed,
pendulum energy, relativistic momentum and energy).  Authored for this gym; used
only by the train slice of the `multiple_consequences` experiment
(`eval/train/build_instances.py`).  Same file format as
`data/systems_and_phenomena`; `Target Polynomial: AUTO` would mean "computed by
elimination onto the measured variables".
